# Direct-source intake

Use this mode only for owner-selected employer, ATS, or third-party job-board URLs outside the LinkedIn numeric-ID sync. It archives one unambiguous job posting and appends one tracker row without changing the external site. Prefer a complete `SourcePostingAcquisitionV2` bundle produced by `acquire-url`; compatible legacy direct records remain supported.

The input is `{"records":[...]}`. Each record supplies `title`, `company`, `location`, `url`, `description`, browser `provenance`, and `quality_evidence` under the normal capture-quality contract. `source_id` is optional; when present it must equal `ext-` plus the first 20 hexadecimal characters of SHA-256 over the canonical URL. Canonicalization lowercases the scheme and host, removes the fragment and tracking query, preserves non-tracking query parameters, and keeps the path. A careers landing page is not a posting unless the captured title and description bind one specific open role.

Run `external-ingest` without `--commit` first. It revalidates V2 selection coverage, bundle and record hashes, embedded quality and every record; checks exact canonical-URL identity across tracker and archive; stages Markdown, source-provenance, compensation and eligibility sidecars plus a workbook copy; and reports the intended rows. A batch with failures, dropped selections, duplicate identities, incomplete descriptions, an Excel lock, or partial tracker/archive state stops. After inspection, add global `--commit`. The commit backs up the tracker, publishes source-bound sidecars, extends `JobsTable`, and verifies readback. It never applies, messages, saves or unsaves anything online.

The current workbook retains its historical `Link puesto linkedin` header. A V2 direct-source row leaves that cell blank and writes the canonical external posting URL to `Link puesto empresa`; the worklist uses the employer URL and binds eligibility evidence to it. `PostingSourceProvenanceV2` separately preserves discovery, final and canonical URLs, provider identity and attempts. Legacy V1 direct-record inputs retain their historical behavior of storing the URL in both columns for compatibility.
